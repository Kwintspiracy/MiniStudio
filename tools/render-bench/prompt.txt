[TMM OVERRIDE INSTRUCTIONS]
Use True Metallic Metal (TMM) acrylic hobby paints for all armor, weapons, and trims. The metals must display a uniform, slightly grainy metallic pigment texture containing mica flakes, with a natural, even metallic sheen. Apply dark liquid washes pooling strictly in the recesses, and light metallic drybrushing catching only the raised textures.
Absolutely no painted, high-contrast matte color gradients, sky-earth reflections, or fake trompe l'oeil light banding on the metal surfaces. The metal must look like standard, single-hue metallic acrylic paint catching a single, realistic light source.

Utilize clean, neutral studio lighting. There are no glowing elements, magical blooms, or Object Source Lighting (OSL) effects on the miniature. Shadows and highlights should be determined strictly by a standard overhead zenithal light source, maintaining consistent local colors without dramatic or vibrant color casts from secondary light sources.
