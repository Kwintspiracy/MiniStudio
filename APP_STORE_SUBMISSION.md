## Description (4000 characters max)

Transform your miniature painting journey with MiniPainterStudio - the
AI-powered visualization tool designed for tabletop hobbyists, wargamers, and
miniature collectors.

VISUALIZE BEFORE YOU PAINT Upload a photo of your unpainted miniature and
instantly see how it could look painted in various professional styles. From
competition-winning Heavy Metal schemes to gritty Grimdark aesthetics, explore
endless possibilities before committing to a single brushstroke.

TWO POWERFUL MODES

Designer Mode

- Generate concept art sketches from reference images
- Create realistic 3D miniature sculpt previews
- Produce studio-quality product photographs
- Perfect for planning conversions and custom projects

Painter Mode

- Apply professional painting styles to your models
- Styles include Heavy Metal, Grimdark, Blanchitsu, SlapChop, and more
- Add advanced effects like Non-Metallic Metal (NMM) and Object Source Lighting
  (OSL)
- See realistic paint applications using actual hobby brand colors

REAL PAINT BRANDS Choose from authentic paint colors including:

- Citadel Colour
- Vallejo
- Army Painter
- Scale75
- Duncan Rhodes Paints

Select your palette and see how those exact colors would look on your miniature.

KEY FEATURES

- Capture photos directly in-app or import from your library
- Multiple painting style presets with customizable prompts
- NMM and OSL effect toggles
- Color palette selection from real paint brands
- Save and share your visualizations
- History gallery to compare results
- Pro mode for enhanced detail generation

PERFECT FOR

- Warhammer 40K & Age of Sigmar painters
- D&D and RPG miniature collectors
- Historical wargamers
- Scale model hobbyists
- Anyone learning to paint miniatures

Stop wondering "what if" and start seeing your vision come to life. Whether
you're a beginner learning color theory or a veteran painter planning your next
masterpiece, MiniPainterStudio helps you paint with confidence.

Download now and transform how you approach miniature painting!

### Notes for Reviewers

MiniPainterStudio is an AI image generation app for the tabletop miniature hobby
community. Users upload photos of unpainted miniatures (game pieces, figurines)
and the app generates visualizations of how they could look when painted.

**Key Technical Notes:**

- The app uses Google Gemini AI for image generation
- All image generation happens server-side via Supabase Edge Functions
- Users authenticate via Google Sign-In or email/password
- No user-generated content is shared publicly
- The app requires an internet connection for AI features

**Testing the App:**

1. Sign in with Google or create an account
2. Take a photo or select an image from your library
3. Choose a painting style (e.g., "Grimdark" or "Heavy Metal")
4. Tap "Create" to generate a painted visualization
5. View results in the Gallery

**Content Notes:**

- Miniature models depicted are fantasy/sci-fi game pieces
- No violent, sexual, or objectionable content is generated
- All AI generations are based on uploaded miniature photos
